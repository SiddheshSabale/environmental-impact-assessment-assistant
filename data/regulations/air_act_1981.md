
# Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981

Official source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/4323/1/air_act_1981.pdf
Additional amendment source: https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2023/248047.pdf
Source note: Check the applicable amendments before relying on these excerpts.

## Relevant provisions

Section 21 — Restrictions on certain industrial plants: No person shall establish or operate any industrial plant in an air pollution control area unless the previous consent of the State Board has been obtained, subject to the provisions and exceptions in the Act.

Section 22 — Emission standards: No person operating any industrial plant in an air pollution control area shall discharge or cause or permit the discharge of any air pollutant in excess of the standards laid down by the State Board under section 17(1)(g).

Section 23 — Information about excess emissions: The Act addresses notification to the State Board and other prescribed authorities when excess emissions occur or are expected due to an accident or other unforeseen event.
