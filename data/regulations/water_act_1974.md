
# Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974

Official source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
Source note: Verify the current amended text before relying on these excerpts.

## Relevant provisions

Section 24 — Prohibition on the use of streams or wells for disposal of polluting matter: The Act addresses the discharge of polluting matter into streams or wells, subject to the provision's scope and exceptions.

Section 25 — Restrictions on new outlets and discharges: The Act addresses restrictions on establishing or taking steps to establish certain outlets or discharges without the previous consent of the State Pollution Control Board, subject to the Act's conditions.

Section 26 — Existing discharges: The Act addresses existing discharges of sewage or trade effluent and related consent requirements under its provisions.
