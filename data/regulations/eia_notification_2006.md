# Environmental Impact Assessment Notification, 2006

**Notification Number:** S.O. 1533(E)  
**Date:** 14 September 2006  
**Issuing Authority:** Government of India, Ministry of Environment and Forests  
**Official source:** https://cpc.parivesh.nic.in/SingleWindow.aspx

## Relevant provisions

**Notification S.O. 1533(E) — Environmental clearance framework:** The notification establishes an environmental clearance framework for specified categories of development projects and activities, subject to its provisions and applicable amendments.

**Schedule — Project categories:** The Schedule identifies project and activity categories covered by the notification. The relevant entry, thresholds, and conditions must be verified from the official notification and applicable amendments before determining whether a project is covered.

**Stage 1 — Screening:** Applies to Category B projects and activities. Screening determines whether a project is classified as B1 or B2 under the applicable provisions.

**Stage 2 — Scoping:** The relevant Expert Appraisal Committee or State Level Expert Appraisal Committee determines the Terms of Reference for the Environmental Impact Assessment study, subject to applicable provisions and exceptions.

**Stage 3 — Public Consultation:** Generally includes a public hearing at or near the project site and obtaining written responses from other concerned persons with a plausible stake in the project's environmental aspects, subject to applicable provisions and exceptions.

**Stage 4 — Appraisal:** The relevant appraisal committee examines the application and applicable EIA documents, including the public consultation outcome where required, before making its recommendation.

**Environmental clearance procedure:** The applicable procedure, competent authority, project category, thresholds, and requirements must be verified against the relevant Schedule entry and applicable amendments.
